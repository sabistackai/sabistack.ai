How we work

Four steps from manual to automatic.

No six-month discovery phase, no slide decks that go nowhere. We learn how your business actually runs, then build what pays back first.

01Weeks 1–2

Audit

Two weeks embedded in your operations, mapping every process and spreadsheet secretly running the business. We sit with the people doing the work — not just the people managing it — using our AI Readiness Audit framework.

What we look at
Every recurring task, who does it and how long it takes
Where data lives: spreadsheets, notebooks, WhatsApp groups, inboxes
Bottlenecks, handoffs and single points of failure
Your team's readiness to adopt new tools
02End of audit

Roadmap

A prioritized list of automations, ranked by hours saved and risk — not by what's easiest for us to build. It's yours to keep, whether you build with us or not.

Sample roadmap
1. Automated daily sales reportHigh impact · Low risk
2. Booking & scheduling systemHigh impact · Med risk
3. Demand forecasting modelMed impact · Later
03Build sprints

Build

The highest-return systems first, in production, with your team using them from week one. We ship in short sprints so you see working software early and steer as we go.

Every sprint includes
A working release your team can use immediately
A demo and feedback session with the people using it
Clear tracking of hours saved against the roadmap
04Handover & beyond

Operate

Documentation, training, and a support window so the systems keep running after we hand them over. You own everything we build — code, accounts and data.

You walk away with
Plain-language runbooks for every system
Trained staff and an internal owner for each tool
A support window, plus optional ongoing care
